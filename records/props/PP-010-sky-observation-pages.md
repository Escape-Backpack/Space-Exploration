## Designer direction - 2026-10-21
The repeating document is a simple sky observation page, with lots of space for the
uncle's notes and drawings. Target size: **4 x 6 inches**. This replaces the former
no-repeating-object direction in PR-002. Page count and distribution are open.

## First layout proposal
AS-010 is a blank portrait draft: 4 inches wide, 6 inches tall. Portrait orientation
is an assistant proposal, not yet selected by the designer.
- Small SKY OBSERVATIONS heading in IBM Plex Mono Medium, 12 pt.
- Date and Time share one row; Location occupies the next. Labels are 8.5 pt.
- Quarter-inch side margins, thin grey rules, dark grey text on unprinted white paper.
- A single large unruled body below the header, without separate writing/drawing boxes.
- No fixed page number, decorative stars, false data, puzzle lines or alignment marks.
- Actual uncle writing and sketches will follow PR-007; the typeset heading and field
  labels are a printed form, not a substitute for his selected handwriting.

The draft is for layout review only. Exact fields, orientation, paper stock and final
artwork are not approved. No observation content or new puzzle answers have been invented.

## Candidate final meta puzzle
The designer suggested small lines on successive observation pages that reveal a
message when the pages are stacked correctly. This is an idea, not a working mechanism.

Opaque paper hides the lower sheets in a flush stack. Possible directions for discussion
are translucent sheets, cutouts, or staggered edges exposing fragments. Holding ordinary
paper to light is another possible experiment, not a verified solution. The intended
paper, number of layers, reveal method, ordering clue, alignment method and final message
remain open. No marks are added until their function is established. A physical prototype
must verify visibility and alignment; screen transparency would not validate paper.

## Player sees
A compact sky-observation form with date, time and location fields, leaving the rest
of the page for the uncle's handwriting and drawings. The current file is blank.

## Production and testing
AS-010 provides an exact-size PDF and a letter-size crop-mark sheet; print at 100% /
Actual size. Digital dimensions and both rendered pages were checked on 2026-10-21.
No physical print, stacking test, playtest, or final text-fit test has been performed.

## Reset
Return pages to their assigned envelopes once distribution is designed. No cutting,
folding or player writing is required by the current blank layout.
