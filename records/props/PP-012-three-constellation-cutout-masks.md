## Earlier Inuit prototype - retained history
Three opaque cards, one per selected constellation (Q-005). Each card is 300 x 180 pt
(4 1/6 x 2.5 inches), with one large counting window and three separate small circular
alignment holes (12 pt diameter, about 4.23 mm). One letter-size sheet holds all three.
Their size is independent of the selected 4 x 6 observation-page format (PP-010).

AS-012 provides cutting outlines. Cut all pieces and openings before play. Small dashed
circles and the main dashed window are removed; the remaining solid outline is the
card edge. Use opaque cardstock; exact stock weight is not selected or tested.

## Player sees
Cards titled TUKTURJUIT / THE CARIBOU, ULLAKTUT / THE RUNNERS, and AAGJUUK / WATCHING
FOR DAYLIGHT. The latter is descriptive wording, not asserted as a translation.
Each card says: "3 small holes on ringed stars. Count the large window."

Put the three small sights over ringed reference stars on PP-011. Only the large
window supplies the count. This prevents reading the answer by counting holes in the
card. The small holes are always three regardless of the answer. Rotation is permitted
in this prototype; keep the printed face up. The digital audit additionally considered
reflected placements. No observation dates or code order are printed on these cards.

## Fit requirements and evidence
The first arbitrary-star alignment attempt produced near-matches, so the prototype uses
visibly ringed reference stars. All nine are candidate alignment points. The geometry
audit verifies exactly one matching reference triple per mask at its stated tolerance,
including different correspondences, rotation and reflection. Count windows expose the
intended selected stars (2, 7 and 3); AS-013 documents the separate digital/physical limits.
No ease-of-discovery claim or physical cutting pass is recorded.
