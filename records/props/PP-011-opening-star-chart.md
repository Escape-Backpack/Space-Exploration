## Earlier Inuit prototype - retained history
One US-letter portrait sheet (8.5 x 11 inches), with three enlarged star fields. It is
an observation reference atlas, not a depiction of a simultaneous sky from a particular
place/date. Each field uses a single gnomonic projection and rigid rotation; all stars
within that field share the same scale and transform. Fields have independent scales.
HYG v4.1 provides positions. Only selected stars are shown; faint neighbours are omitted
from counting regions and very close marks are simplified. No stars are moved to force
a cutout fit. In particular, the seven-star Caribou representation treats Mizar as one
printed mark; its nearby visual companion is omitted from this simplified selection.

AS-011 is the player chart. The three groups are not outlined or named on it. Nine
reference stars have fine rings, three per field. These are the targets of the small
alignment holes in PP-012. Chart labels and footer identify the atlas as selected stars
and separately enlarged fields. No fabricated degree labels or observing date.

This layout is an assistant prototype requested for review, not an approved replacement
for a full-sky map. A single all-sky letter chart would make the small groups harder to
count at this prototype scale. The older AS-002 landscape remains historical concept art.

## Player sees
THE NORTHERN SKY. Three enlarged star fields. Match a mask, then count the stars in
its large window. Small rings distinguish reference stars from other star dots.

## Production

those changes alter matching/counting. Keep the designer key out of the game.
