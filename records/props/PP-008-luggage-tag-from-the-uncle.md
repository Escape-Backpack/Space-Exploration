A luggage tag attached to the backpack. It does several things:
- explains the adventure, with a handwritten welcome from the uncle
- is the first puzzle (PZ-002): the front shows three Inuit constellations in the colour lock's colours
- carries the uncle's handwritten welcome and vintage telescope sketch on the back
- has a planned QR link to the hint page; generation and placement remain pending

The designer approved the following face arrangement and exact note text on
2026-10-05 and selected the original V1 artwork as the luggage tag.
Handwriting follows PR-007. The design is decided; physical dimensions,
print readability and QR placement are not finalized or tested.

- Front: existing coloured constellation artwork, AS-002.
