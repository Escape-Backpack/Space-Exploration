opening, while using cutouts in PZ-002. This is an idea for later in the adventure;
no specific era, position, prerequisite or reuse of a particular chart is selected.

A star chart has degree coordinate lines and some named reference stars, such as
Polaris. The uncle's descriptions direct players to particular regions, where they
find or count stars. His observation pages (PP-010) could carry the descriptions;
that prop assignment remains a possibility, not a production decision.

The designer's example, "the stars on the left of Polaris between 35 and 45 degrees",
is illustrative. Those coordinates do not identify a verified selected constellation.

clues. Supply any knowledge needed to interpret the chart within the game.
