## How it works
A cascade of equations on the uncle's notebook pages (PR-007 style). Each page has one
blank that is filled by the previous page's answer. Chosen over a cross-game thread, which
would let one stuck team block the rest of the game. The puzzle is self-contained.

Illustrative chain, not decided (arithmetic checked):
1. Kepler's third law (T squared = a cubed, years and AU): a planet at 4 AU gives T = 8 years.
2. Angular speed: 360 degrees / 8 years = 45 degrees per year.
3. Position: after 3 years it has moved 135 degrees; the player reads that angle off a
   printed ring, and the symbol or digit there is the answer.

A wrong step gives an untidy number, which tells the player they slipped.

Design guardrails: numbers doable by hand, with the equation and units supplied on the page;
each page says to use the previous result without spelling out the chain; Hiking already had
an equation card, so the discovery is finding the chain, not plugging in numbers.

Open: the real inputs and the answer, the ring design, difficulty, props, and what the
Newton inverse-square idea becomes (a possible second cascade in Beyond).
