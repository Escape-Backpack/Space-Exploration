## Puzzle
The first puzzle. No prerequisite (needs: []). Everything is in the start envelope:
the 5.5 x 8.5 flyer star chart (PP-011, AS-078), the three narrow constellation frames
(PP-012, AS-081) and the uncle's Handlee observation note (PP-010, AS-028).

The note pairs each frame with a constellation and tells which night Bob observed it
(Q-009). Players place each frame on the chart in its one correct position, count the
stars visible through its window, then order the three counts by the nights.
Constellation selection: Q-005.

## Solution
The window counts are Lyra 6, Cygnus 9 and Aquila 8. Ordered by the uncle's three
observing nights (first Lyra, second Cygnus, last Aquila), they give **698**.
Envelopes are numbered with the answer (PR-008), so players open envelope 698.

## Physical test checklist
Digital placement and count checks passed; nothing has been tested on paper.
- One convincing correct placement per frame on the printed chart.
- Front/back orientation, whether frames can rotate, and readable alignment tolerance.
- The window must require counting chart stars, not counting holes in the frame.
- Print and cut the pieces and check star legibility.
