## Designer proposal - 2026-10-21
Choose three constellations from oldest recorded to newest, if that chronology is
available. A text card gives descriptions and perhaps helper drawings; players count
the stars in each. The opening answer would select an envelope directly (PR-008).

## Research result - 2026-10-21
No reliable distinct earliest-record dates for Tukturjuit, Ullaktut and Aagjuuk were
verified in this search. This is a research gap, not proof that such records do not
exist. A publication date does not establish when a constellation tradition originated.
Do not invent dates or describe the traditions as having a proven oldest/newest order.

- [ROM, The Arctic Sky](https://www.rom.on.ca/rom-publications/arctic-sky-inuit-astronomy-star-lore-and-legend): source combines Inuit elder interviews and historical records; the publisher page supplies no three-constellation chronology.
- [University of Alaska Museum, Cultural Star Knowledge](https://www.uaf.edu/museum/education/educators/heliophysics-aurora-outre/activities/pdfs/Cultural-Star-Knowledge-Story-Sheets.pdf), Inuit Astronomy page: names and meanings vary between communities; the examples are attributed to Igloolik. No earliest-record ordering is supplied.
- [Winnipeg Art Gallery, INUA](https://www.wag.ca/inua-online/), artist description of Iluani/Silami: identifies Tukturjuit with the Big Dipper and Ullaktut with Orion's three belt stars. It does not date their first recording.
- [CIRNAC, Inuit](https://rcaanc-cirnac.gc.ca/eng/1100100014187/1534785248701): Inuit are distinct from First Nations. The existing candidates should be described as Inuit, with appropriate regional attribution, if retained.

The full historical source corpus has not been exhaustively reviewed. The existing
Q-005 star-count selection is unchanged; no new count or exact sky geometry is approved.

## Selected ordering - 2026-10-21
The designer accepted the observation-date approach ("That works").
Use the uncle's own dated observation entries for an oldest-to-newest ordering, rather
than earliest historical documentation. These would be fictional story dates, explicitly
his observations, not historical discovery claims; exact dates are not chosen.

Selected ordering approach within the candidate puzzle: match descriptions to depicted star groups, count the stars,
order the counts by the uncle's entries, and open the matching code envelope. The earlier note-order alternative was not selected.
Descriptions should identify the group without stating the answer count. Helper drawings
must not leave star membership ambiguous or reduce the task to copying a printed number.

Still open: confirm the three constellations/community source and choose the actual observation dates,
decide whether text and star field occupy one page or separate cards, and test readability
at 4 x 6 inches. No artwork or new answer has been produced for this revision.
