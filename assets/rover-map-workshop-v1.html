Rules & limits

How terrain combines
  • Sun/shade, radiation and ground type are independent layers.
  • Flat cost: 1 + 1 per 100 g already aboard.
  • Entering uphill: double that entire cost.
  • Entering downhill: 0 battery cost.
  • Then apply the solar gain, capped at max battery.
  • Cliffs cannot be entered. Border arrows are separate from slopes.
  • Revisits are allowed; effects apply on every entry. Samples are collected once and retain damage.

Testing rules
  • Move horizontally or vertically. Revisits are allowed.
  • Pay for entry using destination terrain and current cargo. Pay before any solar credit.
  • Entering radiation affects the rover and samples already aboard.
  • Samples are picked up automatically once; weight counts on the next move. No dropping or damage reset.
  • Reach the finish with all four samples. You may pass through it earlier.
  • A shared start/finish requires leaving and returning with the samples.
  • Undo is a design-testing control.
