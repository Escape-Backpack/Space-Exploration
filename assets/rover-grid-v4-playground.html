Rules for this version
  • Start with 30 battery. Move one square horizontally or vertically. No blocked squares. Revisiting squares is allowed; movement costs and terrain effects apply on every entry.
  • Pay 1 + 1 per 100 g aboard before moving. You must afford the full cost.
  • After entering a sun square, regain 1 battery, capped at 30. Waiting does not recharge.
  • Each sample is picked up once, automatically. Its weight applies to the next move. Revisiting its square does not collect it again or reset its radiation exposure. No dropping samples.
  • Every entered R square adds one exposure to the rover and each sample already aboard. Rover limit: 2; each sample: 1.
  • Arrowed borders can only be crossed in the indicated direction. Other open borders work both ways.
  • Finish with all four samples. Undo and reset are testing controls.
